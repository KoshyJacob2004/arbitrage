ARBITRAGECALC.JS

“arbitrageCalc.js is where I keep the actual math...

The first function, parseAmericanOdds, cleans up the odds values...

Then americanToDecimal converts American odds into decimal odds...

The small helper functions like formatAmericanOdds, money, and percent are just there to make the output look clean...

The main function is arbitrageCalc...

Then it returns all the important numbers: the stake for each side, the possible payouts, guaranteed payout, guaranteed profit, ROI, and total implied probability.”
