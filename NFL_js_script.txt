NFL.JS

“NFL.js is the NFL version of the scraper...

It opens the page with Puppeteer, clicks into the moneyline section, grabs the top and bottom rows from the odds table, and pulls out each team’s sportsbook odds...

Once it has the odds, it loops through the matchups and compares sportsbook combinations to see if any pair creates an arbitrage opportunity...

This file is more of an extension of the project.”
